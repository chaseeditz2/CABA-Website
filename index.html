01 ABOUT CABA

MORE THAN A TEAM. ONE FORMATION.

Combined Arms Blue Angels, known as CABA, is a Roblox aviation demonstration team inspired by the precision and discipline of professional aerial demonstration flying.

Every member of CABA shares equal ownership and responsibility for the team's success. Together, the team represents coordination, dedication, discipline, and trust.

PRIMARY PLATFORM

F/A-18C HORNET

The F/A-18C Hornet serves as the aircraft at the center of the CABA demonstration experience.

Built for precision maneuvering and close formation flying, the Hornet allows the team to perform dynamic aerial demonstrations across Roblox aviation environments.
